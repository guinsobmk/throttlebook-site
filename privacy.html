Throttlebook

Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

Operator and contact

Throttlebook is made by Boumora Labs. Contact throttlebook.support@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Your garage stays on your device

Bikes, VINs, odometer readings, service and fuel entries, costs, notes, reminders, settings and attached photos are stored locally. We do not operate a server that receives this garage data. You can export it, delete records or remove the app. Device backups may include app data according to Apple or Google settings. Files you export or share go to the destination you choose.

Android advertising

Android includes Google AdMob. Test builds can use Google test ads. When ads are requested, Google's SDK may collect and share advertising/device identifiers, IP address and approximate location inferred from it, app or ad interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Your garage records are not sent to AdMob.

The app uses Google's consent flow before initializing ads and requests ads only when that flow permits it. Where a privacy-options entry point is required, Settings offers Ad privacy choices. Android settings also allow advertising ID controls. See Google's privacy policy and Google's information about partner apps.

iPhone purchases

The iPhone configuration excludes the advertising SDK. Where Pro purchases are configured, Apple handles payment and RevenueCat verifies purchases. RevenueCat may receive an app customer identifier, purchase/receipt information, store and technical information and IP address. The app starts purchase services when you open Pro or restore purchases, then checks on later launches for users whose store connection has been established. This supports entitlements, renewals, cancellation and refunds. We do not receive your card details. See RevenueCat's policy and Apple's policy.

Catalogue pictures and recalls

Catalogue picture downloads from Wikimedia Commons send ordinary network information, including IP address. Downloads default to Wi-Fi, with controls in Settings. User-requested US recall checks send make, model and model year to NHTSA, not the VIN. Those services receive the connecting IP address. See Wikimedia's policy and NHTSA's policy.

Camera, photos and notifications

Camera/photo access is requested when you add photos. Reminders are local notifications; there is no app push server. The app does not request device location permission, although advertising services may infer approximate location from IP.

Other processing and your choices

The app has no developer analytics or crash-reporting SDK; advertising measurement is described above. External links open in your browser. Contacting support sends the information you choose to include in your email. Third-party providers control their own processing locations and retention under their policies; deleting local records does not necessarily delete their records. Contact us for help with privacy requests and provide only the information needed to identify the issue.

Audience and changes

Throttlebook is a tool for motorcycle owners. Store audience declarations are reviewed separately. Questions about suitability or privacy can be sent to support. Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised effective date.
