Throttlebook: Motorcycle Log

A motorcycle garage and service log with maintenance reminders, fuel and cost records, photos and exports. Core garage data is stored on your device. No account is required.

Android is free with ads. The iPhone version has Free and Pro features; purchase availability depends on store configuration. This page does not promise a released build or a particular price.

Support

Email throttlebook.support@gmail.com. Include your app version, phone model and a description of the issue. Please do not send passwords, payment details or private documents.

Moving your garage

In Settings, choose Back up everything. Save the file where you can access it on the new phone, then choose Restore from a backup. Backups may contain your photos and private records; choose their destination carefully.

iPhone Pro purchases

Where purchases are enabled, use the same Apple Account and choose Restore purchases. Subscriptions are managed in your iPhone Settings under Apple Account, Subscriptions. Android currently has no subscription to cancel.
